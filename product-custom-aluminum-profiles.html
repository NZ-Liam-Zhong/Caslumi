Die development

Tooling is where projects are won or lost

A badly designed die produces a profile that is expensive forever. We would rather spend a week on the cross-section than a year fighting it in production.

Before we cut anything, our engineers review your drawing for wall thickness balance, tongue ratios, corner radii and the finish you are asking the surface to hold. Where a small change will meaningfully reduce cost or scrap, we show you the alternative and let you decide.

Because the die library is ours, a repeat order in year three runs off the same tool as the first one. There is no re-tooling surprise when you re-order.

  • Die design reviewed against your drawing before cutting
  • Tooling cut in-house, not outsourced and queued
  • First samples produced on the production die, not a prototype
  • Dies catalogued, stored and maintained for repeat runs
