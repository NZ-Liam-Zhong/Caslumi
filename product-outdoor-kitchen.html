Design for modularity

Build the range once, then extend it cheaply

The expensive mistake in outdoor kitchen programmes is tooling every SKU separately. We would rather design your extrusion set so next season's additions reuse it.

01

Shared extrusion set

One family of profiles carries the whole cabinet range, so new widths cost a saw setting rather than a new die.

02

Appliance-agnostic cut-outs

Cut-out patterns are punched from programme data, so switching your grill supplier does not scrap the carcass.

03

Flat-pack or built

Ship assembled for contract installs, or flat-packed to cut container cost on retail assortments.
