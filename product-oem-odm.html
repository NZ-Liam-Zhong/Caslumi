How a programme runs

Eight steps, no surprises

Drawing or idea

DWG, STEP, PDF, a physical sample or a reference photo — whatever you have is enough to start.

Feasibility review

Engineering checks extrudability, wall thickness, welding access, finish behaviour and assembly logic.

Quotation

Tooling cost, unit price, MOQ and lead time, itemised so you can see what drives the number.

Mold & prototype

Die cut, fixtures built, first articles produced on production tooling.

Sample approval

You review, measure and test. Changes are made before the line is committed.

Mass production

Scheduled across the plants best suited to the process mix and your delivery date.

Quality inspection

Incoming, in-process, welding, dimensional, finish, packaging and pre-shipment checks.

Shipping

Packed, documented and despatched from China or Cambodia, whichever suits your trade plan.
