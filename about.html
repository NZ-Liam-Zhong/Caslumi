Who we are

A manufacturing group, presented the way U.S. buyers need to see it

Behind Caslumi sit seven production plants in Guangdong, China, along with furniture assembly and finished-goods capacity in Cambodia. Thousands of people work across the network.

The plants have been making aluminum profiles, outdoor furniture components and finished furniture for years. What they have not had is a front door built for the way American companies buy — clear specifications, straight answers, realistic lead times, and a person who replies.

That is what Caslumi is. Not a layer of markup between you and the factory, but the commercial and engineering interface that makes the factory usable.

What we do

Four questions, answered plainly

The things a sourcing manager actually needs to know before deciding whether to spend an hour on a call.

What we make

Custom aluminum profiles, outdoor furniture frames and components, fire pit table structures, outdoor kitchen and grill island cabinetry, HDPE and mixed-material furniture, and complete aluminum outdoor furniture — on an OEM or ODM basis.

Where our supply chain is

Aluminum extrusion, machining, welding, finishing and mold development in Guangdong, China. Furniture assembly and finished-goods production in Cambodia, giving an alternative export origin.

Which market we serve

Primarily the United States: outdoor furniture brands, furniture manufacturers, importers and distributors, retailers, hospitality groups and private-label companies.

Why work with us

Because we own the processes rather than brokering them, because engineering input arrives before tooling instead of after, and because two countries of manufacture give you options a single factory cannot.
