Why buy components rather than furniture

Keep your assembly. Fix your metal supply.

A lot of our best customers already have factories. What they do not have is an upstream supplier who can extrude a new section and hold the geometry across a season.

Kit by assembly, not by box

Components arrive grouped by the unit they build, so your line is not sorting parts before it starts working.

One supplier, many processes

Extrusion, machining, welding and coating under one quotation instead of four vendors blaming each other.

Tooling stays with your programme

Your dies and fixtures are catalogued and stored, so a repeat order in year three still matches year one.

Finish in China or raw for you

Take parts coated and ready, or mill finish if your own coating line sets the colour standard.

Volume without drama

More than 3,000 workers across our manufacturing network mean a seasonal spike is a scheduling question, not a crisis.
