04

Gas & ethanol burner kits

Burner assemblies in 304 stainless steel, supplied as a kit with regulator and ignition. These are the parts a buyer cannot afford to get wrong, so they are specified by certificate rather than by description.

Gas burner kits

  • Square burner — 45,000–55,000 BTU, 16"–19"
  • Round burner — 37,000–55,000 BTU, 13"–25"
  • Rectangular burner — 45,000–65,000 BTU, to 36"×8"
  • 304 stainless steel construction
  • Electronic ignitor or match-lit
  • 1,200 mm regulator and hose
  • Natural gas or LPG configuration

Ethanol burner inserts

  • Square ethanol burner
  • Round ethanol burner
  • Conical ethanol burner
  • Circular tabletop burner
  • 304 stainless steel construction

Compliance

Gas-burning products sold in the United States carry certification obligations. Certification for burner and control assemblies is held at component level and confirmed programme by programme — ask us for the certificate number and issuing body covering the exact assembly you are quoting, and do the same with every other supplier you speak to.
