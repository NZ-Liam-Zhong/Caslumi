Why aluminum

It does not rust, it is light enough to ship economically, and it can be extruded into exactly the section your designer drew instead of a section someone else already stocks.

Frame-first engineering

Because we make the frame, we can reduce weld joints, consolidate parts and take weight out where the load does not need it — which shows up in your landed cost.

Finish longevity

Pre-treatment and coating are specified for the environment the furniture will live in, not a generic indoor standard.
