China · Guangdong

The metalworking base

Guangdong is where aluminum outdoor furniture is actually made, and our plants sit in the middle of that supply base — close to billet suppliers, coating lines, hardware makers and the port.

This is where the difficult work happens: cutting dies, running presses, holding tolerances on machined components, welding frames that have to stay square, and finishing surfaces that have to survive five years of sun and salt.

  • Aluminum extrusion on our own presses
  • CNC machining and metal parts production
  • Frame welding and fabrication
  • Furniture component manufacturing
  • Surface finishing — powder coat, anodizing, wood-grain transfer
  • Die and mold development, catalogued for repeat orders

Cambodia

The finished-goods base

Cambodia gives our customers a second route to market — an alternative origin for finished furniture, with a different duty, logistics and lead-time profile.

For most programmes the split is natural: metalwork and components are produced in Guangdong where the process capability lives, then assembled and packed into finished goods in Cambodia. For others, everything runs from China. The right answer depends on your product, your volumes and your trade planning — and we will walk through it with you honestly rather than pushing whichever plant needs the work.

  • Furniture assembly lines
  • Finished furniture production
  • Export flexibility on country of origin
  • Retail-ready and drop-ship packing

Why this matters to a U.S. buyer

Options are the product

Single-factory sourcing works right up until the moment it does not — a tariff change, a capacity crunch, a quality problem, a shipping season. Then it becomes very expensive very quickly.

Origin planning

Decide where finished goods ship from based on your trade position, not your supplier's limitations.

Capacity headroom

Seasonal peaks are absorbed across plants instead of pushing your delivery date out.

Cost routing

Process-heavy parts stay in China; labour-heavy assembly can move. You see both quotes.

Continuity

If one node has a problem, the programme does not stop — the tooling and the drawings sit with the group.
