Custom frame development

Aluminum sofa frame for a U.S. outdoor furniture brand

The customer had a design they liked and a landed cost they could not hit with their existing vendor.

Brief

Deep-seat sofa frame, teak-look armrest detail, five-year coastal warranty.

Engineering

Profile redesigned to cut wall thickness where load allowed and consolidate two extrusions into one.

Result

Fewer weld joints, lower part count, and a frame that passed the brand's corrosion testing.
