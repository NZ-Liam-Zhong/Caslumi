What we actually sell

We are not a catalogue. We are the factory floor behind it.

Most suppliers can sell you a chair. Far fewer can take your drawing, cut the die, extrude the profile, machine and weld the frame, finish it, and pack it for a U.S. container.

That full path — aluminum billet to finished furniture — is what our seven plants in Guangdong and our assembly capacity in Cambodia exist to do. If you are developing a new outdoor collection, replacing an unreliable vendor, or need a component nobody else will tool up for, that is exactly the conversation we want.

Custom frame development

Aluminum sofa frame for a U.S. outdoor furniture brand

The customer had a design they liked and a landed cost they could not hit with their existing vendor.

Brief

Deep-seat sofa frame, teak-look armrest detail, five-year coastal warranty.

Engineering

Profile redesigned to cut wall thickness where load allowed and consolidate two extrusions into one.

Result

Fewer weld joints, lower part count, and a frame that passed the brand's corrosion testing.
