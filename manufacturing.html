Eleven processes

What we can do to a piece of aluminum

Any combination of these can sit inside one quotation, with one person accountable for the result.

Extrusion

In-network presses running 6000-series furniture and industrial profiles, from simple tube to multi-cavity sections.

Cutting

Saw cutting to length, angle and mitre in production volume, with length control checked through the run.

Drilling

Jig-based hole patterns so the hundredth component mates exactly like the first one did.

Punching

High-repeat punched features and cut-outs, tooled per programme for speed and consistency.

Bending

Curved arms, backs, rails and hoops formed to your radius without collapsing the section.

CNC machining

Pockets, slots, tapped holes and mating features machined on components and hardware.

Welding

TIG and MIG frame welding on fixtures, so geometry repeats across an entire production run.

Anodizing

Anodized finishes for programmes where a coating layer is not the right answer.

Mold development

New extrusion dies cut in-house in about five days, then catalogued for repeat orders.

Assembly

Sub-assembly or complete furniture build, run in China or Cambodia depending on your plan.

Custom packaging

Carton design, foam tooling, retail-ready and drop-ship packs, private-label print and labelling.
