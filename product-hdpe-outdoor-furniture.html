Material choice

When HDPE is the right answer

It is not always. But for the right programme it removes a warranty problem that wood and coated steel never stop causing.

No refinishing

Colour runs through the section, so scratches and sun exposure do not expose a different substrate underneath.

Pool and coastal duty

Chlorine and salt air are not a problem for HDPE, which makes it easy to warranty in the environments that kill other materials.

Aluminum where it counts

Combining an aluminum frame with HDPE surfaces gives you structural span without the weight and cost of thick plastic sections.

Deciding between materials?

Tell us the price point, the warranty you need to offer and where the product will live. We will tell you which build makes sense.
