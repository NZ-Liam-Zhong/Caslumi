Compliance — read this carefully

The gas side is certified, and we can prove it

Gas-burning products sold in the United States carry certification obligations, and a supplier who waves those away is a supplier who will cost you a recall.

Our burner kits are CSA approved, each to its own certificate number, and our gas products carry Kiwa EU type-examination for CE marking and AGA certification for Australia. More usefully, our own gas testing laboratory is qualified by CSA Group as a Testing Facility under ISO/IEC 17025:2017, so certification testing can be witnessed on our site instead of shuttling samples between continents.

What that does not mean is that any enclosure you draw is automatically covered. Certification applies to a defined burner, valve train, enclosure and gas type. We will tell you before tooling which of your design decisions trigger re-examination and how long that adds.
