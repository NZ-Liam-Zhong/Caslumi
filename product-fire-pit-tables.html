Compliance — read this carefully

We are honest about what we certify

Gas-burning products sold in the United States carry certification obligations, and a supplier who waves those away is a supplier who will cost you a recall.

Our scope is the manufactured structure and its assembly. Certification of the burner and control assembly normally sits with the gas component manufacturer, and the listing for the finished appliance is agreed programme by programme.

Tell us which standard and listing route your programme requires and we will confirm in writing, before tooling, exactly which parts of it we can carry and which parts stay with your nominated component supplier.
